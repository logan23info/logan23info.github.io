The security engineering lifecycle#

Security is not a checklist — it is a lifecycle that runs continuously. Every domain in this wiki maps to a phase of that lifecycle. This page shows how they fit together.

How the domains connect#

Design feeds Build#

Threat models produced during design (STRIDE, AI Threat Modelling, ICS Threat Modelling) define the security requirements that Secure Architecture, Cloud Security, and Cryptography then implement.

Build feeds Operate#

The controls you build must be monitored. Detection Engineering creates the detections, Threat Intelligence informs what to look for, and Purple Teaming validates that detections actually fire.

Operate feeds Respond#

When detections fire, Incident Response takes over — SOC Playbooks and IR Playbooks turn alerts into coordinated action.

Respond feeds Govern#

Every incident produces a Post-Incident Review whose lessons update Metrics, inform Risk Appetite, and feed Compliance evidence.

Govern feeds Design#

Advisory & Assurance and Compliance findings become new requirements that feed back into the next design cycle — closing the loop.
